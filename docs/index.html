Stop losing 3% of every invoice to credit card processing fees. ClauTime auto-generates invoices from your tracked hours and sends them through Stripe with ACH bank transfer, so you keep nearly all of what you earned.

The card-fee tax on contractors

Most invoicing tools push your client to pay with a credit card. That's convenient for them and expensive for you. On a typical $5,000 monthly invoice, credit card fees eat $145 before you see a dollar.

Stripe's ACH bank-transfer rate is capped at 0.8% / $5 max per charge. ClauTime's invoice flow has a one-click ACH-only toggle that removes the credit card option entirely, so clients pay via bank transfer by default and you keep the difference.

  • Auto-generated line items from your billable Claude Code sessions
  • AI-written descriptions for each day so clients see what they're paying for
  • Test/sandbox mode to dry-run invoices before sending
  • Auto-sync of paid/open status — no manual reconciliation
  • Hosted invoice URL and downloadable PDF, branded with your Stripe account

Zero configuration required

ClauTime reads what Claude Code already writes. No setup, no integrations, no overhead.

01

Claude Code writes logs

Every conversation is recorded as JSONL files in ~/.claude/projects/. This happens automatically — it's how Claude Code works.

02

ClauTime detects sessions

Smart gap-based detection with tool-aware idle timeouts. Tracks prompts, tokens, models, and timing across all your projects.

03

You bill and get paid

Assign sessions to clients, generate timesheets, and send Stripe invoices with one click — ACH-only if you want to skip the credit card fees.
